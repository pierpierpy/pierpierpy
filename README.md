## 🧠 About Me

- 💼 **Senior Data Scientist** at Fastweb's AI Center of Excellence in Milan
- 🤖 Work on post-training large language models — specifically **MIIA**, Fastweb's Italian LLM — using LoRA SFT, DPO, GRPO, and Generative Adversarial Distillation on a 128-GPU DGX H100 cluster
- 🔬 Research on "Using ML to Uncover Subjective Incentives in Executive Compensation" (Bocconi)
- 📚 I love learning by reading — and by re-inventing the wheel, building things from scratch
