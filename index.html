About Me

I'm Piergiorgio Di Pasquale, a Senior Data Scientist at Fastweb's AI Center of Excellence in Milan. I work on post-training large language models — specifically MIIA, Fastweb's Italian LLM — using techniques like LoRA SFT, DPO, GRPO, and Generative Adversarial Distillation on a 128-GPU DGX H100 cluster.

I love learning by reading books, studying, and re-inventing the wheel. I'm passionate about building things from scratch — whether that's a lightweight experiment tracking tool, a VLM pipeline for SEC filings, or synthetic data pipelines for function calling.

Active open-source contributor with projects published on PyPI and HuggingFace. Currently based in Milan, Italy.
